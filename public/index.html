Setup instructions
  1. Add APIFY_API_KEY to your .env file
  2. Open app.apollo.io and filter by your ICP: Founder, MD, Managing Director, CEO in India, 21–500 employees (proxy for ₹30–80cr)
  3. Copy the URL from your browser and paste it above, or click "Use pre-built ICP URL"
  4. Set max leads (50 is a good start), click Generate
  5. Wait ~2-4 min for Apify to scrape. Leads appear below scored by ICP fit.
  6. Click Load into Chain → on any lead to pre-fill the Agent Chain
